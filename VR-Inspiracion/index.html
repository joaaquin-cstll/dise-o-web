Virgen de los Reyes

Upcoming Tour Listen Now

Over more than four decades, the band has been honored as an Honorary Brother (Hermano Honorífico) by brotherhoods such as San Esteban, La Resurrección, and Los Dolores de Torreblanca, while also expanding its festive presence in the city with its traditional Christmas repertoire alongside the Heraldo Real.

Following the leadership transition to Antonio Velasco in 2020 upon Ramírez's retirement, the ensemble has taken on new projects, including forming a brass quintet for Las Siete Palabras, performing in the 2023 Santo Entierro Grande, and joining brotherhoods like El Divino Perdón. Throughout its history, the band has recorded a total of 16 albums (13 of processional marches and 3 featuring Christmas and children's music).

Biography

Founded on May 30, 1980, within the Hermandad de Jesús Despojado and driven by the leadership of Juan Antonio Ramírez Téllez, the band—known independently since 1992 as Agrupación Musical Nuestra Señora de los Reyes—quickly established itself in Seville's Holy Week, accompanying brotherhoods such as La Sed, La Resurrección, Los Gitanos, and San Esteban. Its history was defined in 1988 by the premiere of the arrangement of "La Saeta", which became a milestone in cofrade tradition alongside the Señor de la Salud.
